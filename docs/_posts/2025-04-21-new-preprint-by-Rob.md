Congratulations to Robert McDonald on his [new preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.15442) on using TDA for model selection and parameter inference in tumour-induced angiogenesis! This is joint work with [Dr. Helen Byrne](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/people/helen.byrne), [Dr. Heather Harrington](https://www.mpi-cbg.de/research/researchgroups/currentgroups/heather-harrington/research), and [Dr. Thomas Thorne](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/people/tom-thorne).
